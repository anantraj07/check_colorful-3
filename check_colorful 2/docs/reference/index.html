      ✓ Key takeaways

        Mean time to disposal (working days)

        Cooler colours = shorter disposal time; warmer colours = longer disposal time.

        Throughput H (hearings / working day)

        Bubble area is proportional to mean throughput. Zero throughput has zero area.

        How to read this plot

        1. Position: each bubble sits at its mean hearings per case (complexity, X) and mean hearings per case-month (service rate, S).
        2. Bubble size: larger bubbles mean more hearings are held per working day for that case type (throughput).
        3. Colour: warmer colours mean a longer mean time to disposal.
        4. Guide lines: dashed lines show implied duration: mean complexity ÷ mean service rate. These are reference ratios, not observed disposal times.
